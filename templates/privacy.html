privacy

Privacy

This website is a plain, static site. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other servers. This page explains the little personal data that is still involved.

controller

This website belongs to the Interactive Programming & Analysis Lab, part of the Research Unit of Software Engineering (E194-01) at TU Wien. The controller within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is the Rectorate of TU Wien, Karlsplatz 13, 1040 Vienna, Austria.

For questions about this website, contact Jürgen Cito.

data protection officer

TU Wien's data protection officer can be reached at datenschutz@tuwien.ac.at (TU Wien, Karlsplatz 13/018, 1040 Vienna). Please send requests concerning your rights as a data subject to this address.

hosting & log files

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit a page, your browser sends technical data to GitHub's servers, as with any website: your IP address, the date and time, the requested page, and your browser's user agent. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they have a GitHub account. We have no access to these logs and do not receive any visitor statistics.

This processing is necessary to deliver the website and keep it secure. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub may process this data in the USA. For details and retention periods, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

no cookies or tracking

We do not use cookies, local storage, analytics, tracking pixels, social media plugins, or embedded content from other providers. Fonts, photos and scripts are all served from this website itself. The small scripts on the site (cycling research questions and the publication filter) run only in your browser and send no data anywhere.

people on this site

The Team page lists the lab's members with their name, role, work email, office and phone number, and photo. This information comes from TU Wien's information system TISS, where staff decide themselves whether to provide a photo. Publication lists come from the public bibliography DBLP. Publishing staff names, photos and publication lists on the website of a research institution is permitted under § 2h(1)(1) of the Austrian Research Organisation Act (FOG). Lab members may object to the publication of their photo at any time.

contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message like any other email, and use them to reply to you. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), or Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your request concerns a contract, such as a thesis or a position.

links to other sites

This site links to other websites, for example publishers, TISS, or GitHub. If you follow such a link, the other site's privacy policy applies; we have no influence on how it handles your data.

your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. To exercise these rights, contact datenschutz@tuwien.ac.at.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde, dsb@dsb.gv.at).

See also TU Wien's general data protection information and legal notice.
