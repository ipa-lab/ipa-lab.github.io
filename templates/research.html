research

Play and exploration are how real systems get built.

We see the elements of play and exploration in activities of software and data engineering as an essential part of developing larger computational systems. We look at them through two lenses.

two lenses

  1. 01
    Empirical Understanding. We want to understand the underlying structures and artifacts that govern exploration and experimentation in computational tasks.
  2. 02
    System Building. We design, build, and evaluate interventions in the form of systems that either automate or augment human ability to better deal with these structures and artifacts.
